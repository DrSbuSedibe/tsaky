Sea moss, also known as Irish moss or Chondrus crispus, is a type of red algae that grows along the Atlantic coasts of Europe, North America, and South Africa. This remarkable seaweed has been used for centuries as a natural remedy and nutritional supplement.

Sea moss is often called "nature's multivitamin" because it contains over 90 of the 102 minerals that the human body needs. It's rich in iodine, calcium, potassium, magnesium, and many other essential nutrients that support overall health and wellness.

Take 1-2 tablespoons of sea moss gel daily as part of a balanced diet. You can add it to:

  • Smoothies and juices
  • Coffee or tea
  • Yoghurt and oatmeal
  • Protein shakes
  • Any recipe of your choice

It's versatile and easy to incorporate into your daily routine. Start with a smaller amount and gradually increase to allow your body to adjust.

Refrigeration: Store sea moss gel in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 weeks. Always use a clean spoon when serving to maintain freshness.

Freezing: For longer storage, freeze sea moss gel in ice cube trays for up to 3 months. This is perfect for portion control!

Important: Always check the expiration date on your jar and follow proper storage guidelines for best results.

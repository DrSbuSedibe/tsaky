What is Sea Moss?

Sea moss, also known as Irish moss or Chondrus crispus, is a type of red algae that grows along the Atlantic coasts of Europe, North America, and South Africa. This remarkable seaweed has been used for centuries as a natural remedy and nutritional supplement.

Sea moss is often called "nature's multivitamin" because it contains over 90 of the 102 minerals that the human body needs. It's rich in iodine, calcium, potassium, magnesium, and many other essential nutrients that support overall health and wellness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sea moss, also known as Irish moss, is a type of red algae that grows along the Atlantic coasts of Europe and North America. It's packed with essential minerals and nutrients, making it a popular superfood. Our sea moss gel is made from wildcrafted sea moss, carefully prepared to preserve its natural benefits.

Take 1-2 tablespoons of sea moss gel daily as part of a balanced diet. You can add it to smoothies, juices, coffee, tea, yoghurt, oatmeal, or any recipe of your choice. It's versatile and easy to incorporate into your daily routine.

Store your sea moss gel in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 weeks. Always use a clean spoon when serving to maintain freshness. For longer storage, you can freeze it in ice cube trays for up to 3 months.
